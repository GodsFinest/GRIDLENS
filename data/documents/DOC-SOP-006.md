# DOC-SOP-006: Feeder Trip Investigation & Auto-Reclose Standard Operating Procedure

## Section 1: Immediate Field Actions Following a Feeder Trip
1. Verify breaker status (CB12 / CB13) on substation SCADA single-line diagram.
2. Download SOE event logs and COMTRADE oscillography records from the primary protection relay.
3. Compare relay target flags (e.g. 51P, 50N) with waveform oscillography.
4. Execute insulation resistance (megger) test on the tripped feeder cable before attempting reclosure.
5. If waveform analysis reveals uncoordinated tripping or channel mapping discrepancies, suspend auto-reclosing and initiate secondary wiring audit.
