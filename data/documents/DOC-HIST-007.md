# DOC-HIST-007: Substation Historical Incident Archive (2024-2025)

## Section 1: Incident Log - Feeder F12
- **INC-2025-089 (2025-11-14)**: Phase C-to-Ground fault on Feeder F12. Current reached 3720 A RMS. Cause: Tree branch contact during heavy wind storm. Clearing time: 56 ms. Breaker CB12 operated correctly.
- **INC-2025-034 (2025-04-10)**: Feeder F12 tripped on instantaneous overcurrent (50P). Cause: Underground cable splice insulation breakdown at km 4.2. Current reached 4100 A.

## Section 2: Historical Wiring Issues
- **INC-2024-012 (2024-08-22)**: Commissioning maintenance on Bay F12 resulted in inverted secondary wiring between Phase A and Phase C CT test blocks. Relay reported Phase A trip while oscillography showed physical fault on Phase C. Remediated via wiring rewire.
